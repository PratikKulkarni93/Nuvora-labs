How long does a project take?

Many websites and automations are ready in 3 to 7 days, and most apps in 1 to 2 weeks. Bigger projects follow the timeline you need, and we plan the work to meet it.

Yes. When the project is paid for, the code and all assets are yours.

Yes. We integrate with CRMs, accounting tools, email, WhatsApp, spreadsheets and custom APIs.
